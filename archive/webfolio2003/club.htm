Clubs

  • All Club dates will run on a five-period schedule.

  • Clubs will meet on each club date unless there is a substitute for that day.

  • Students who don not have a 2.0 in the specific club meeting period  should not be dismissed to attend a club meeting

  • No tests on club days

  • Teachers will give temporary pass for first meeting. The sponsor should issue permanent club passes/membership cards.

  • Students must report to their classes for attendance and assignments before they may be excused to their club meeting.

  • Advisors are to keep students for entire meeting time.
